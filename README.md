# The EM × AI Field Guide · Case Studies Edition

Combined edition: the 18 KEEN entrepreneurial-mindset habits (EM × AI Field
Guide) with field evidence from the iFLY wind tunnel case study — 65 engineer
interview clips, design stories, and 5 classroom-ready teaching modules.

## Opening the site

- **Best:** double-click `Start Field Guide.command` — it serves the site
  locally and opens it in your browser. This makes the iFLY YouTube clips
  play inline. (YouTube refuses to run its embedded player on pages opened
  directly from disk — that's their Error 153 — so the site needs to be
  served over HTTP for inline playback.)
- Opening `em-ai-fieldguide.html` directly from disk also works; YouTube
  clips then open on youtube.com in a new tab instead of playing inline.

## What's inside

- All 18 habit pages are complete regardless of iFLY coverage; iFLY evidence
  appears only where a natural mapping exists in the source material.
- The case study is location-independent: modules and clips run in any
  classroom. No visit to an iFLY facility is required or implied.
- Seven case studies in all: the iFLY wind tunnel case (65 clips, 5 teaching
  modules) plus six coded KEEN cases — Skycam, Sportvision, Christini, IDEO,
  TwistJect, and Mighty Mug. Each of the six has its own case page with a
  habit-profile graph and bar chart, and timestamped evidence you can jump
  to. Habit pages carry evidence pulled from across all seven cases, not
  just iFLY, so a habit's field evidence may span several case studies.
- Deep links: `#habit/systems-thinking`, `#ifly/module/bernoulli/step/6`,
  `#ifly/clips`, `#case/<id>`, `#case/<id>/t/<seconds>`, `#fieldmap`, and
  any view id.
- Single self-contained HTML file; habit explainer videos live in
  `notebooklm_videos/`; iFLY clips stream from YouTube on click (no
  third-party requests until a clip is played).
- Built from: the original EM × AI Field Guide site, `modules_chain.json`,
  and the em-vault clip notes. The originals are unmodified.
